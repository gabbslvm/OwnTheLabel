02 — THE BEGINNING

It started with an idea.

Own The Label began in 2025 with Jabez Celestino, a student who collected hoodies and tees from local brands. He loved the designs, the creativity, and the stories behind them, and asked one question: what if apparel could be customized as freely as Crocs with Jibbitz?

He first called it “Make Your Brand.” When he shared the idea with Jasmine, she suggested “Own The Label.” It just felt right.

She validated the first designs and pushed to make it real. Together they built the brand on one promise: the same brand, but never the same look.

03 — WHAT WE DO

Wear your story.

We make comfortable shirts, hoodies, and other essentials that you can personalize with embroidered, chenille, and other unique patches. It's not just about wearing clothes. It's about wearing your story, your creativity, and your individuality.
